👀How a satellite🛰️ orbits Earth🌍💖😍

🌍Earth's gravity pulls the satellite inward.
Its sideways velocity keeps it moving forward, so it continually falls around Earth. This drawing is a simplified circular orbit, not to scale.

Orbital speed: One orbit:

r = R + h   v = √(GM/r)   T = 2π√(r³/GM)

Here
R is Earth's radius,
h is altitude,
M is Earth's mass,
G is the gravitational constant.
The satellite's own mass cancels out.
